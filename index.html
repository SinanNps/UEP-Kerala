KERALA · PROGRAMME PROPOSAL

Urban Employment Programme

A proposed three-month apprenticeship programme connecting eligible young residents of Kerala’s Urban Local Bodies with workplace opportunities, while responding to staffing shortages in urban institutions.

Start demo application

Problem statement

GRADUATE UNEMPLOYMENT
11.3%

Urban unemployment rate among graduates in Kerala, compared with 10.6% nationally.

Source cited in proposal: PLFS 2025.
URBAN LOCAL BODY STAFFING

Shortages across key functions

The proposal identifies staffing shortages in Finance, Technical, Management, Administrative, Legal and Planning functions.

Source cited in proposal: Kerala Urban Policy 2026–2050.
Programme design in the PDF

Three-month apprenticeship; ₹10,900 fixed monthly stipend; ₹2,000 monthly Employment Enhancer Incentive from the State; training through ASAP Skill Portal and on-the-job training (OJT). Apprenticeship may convert to a job after three months by mutual agreement.

Reduced exam set: three proposed clusters

The PDF lists seven sectors. The following related groupings are prototype design choices to reduce the number of exam tracks; they are not groupings stated in the PDF.

Four exam cycles and ranked lists

Four illustrative 2027 cycles are included. Each cycle creates a separate merit list for each cluster. Hosts select from the relevant list, matching the vacancy's cluster, role module and location. Exam dates, list validity, cut-off and tie-break rules require formal approval.

Eligibility in the PDF

Residence

Resident of any Urban Local Body in Kerala.

Qualification

Bachelor’s degree, ITI or Polytechnic from a recognised institution.

Age

24 years of age or younger at the time of application.
