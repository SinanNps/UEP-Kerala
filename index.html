Age

04 / APPRENTICESHIP TERMS

Financial support
during the pathway.

Monthly support combines the fixed apprenticeship stipend and the State's Employment Enhancer Incentive. A transition allowance is also proposed.

FIXED MONTHLY STIPEND₹10,900

Per apprentice, per month, for the three-month apprenticeship.

EMPLOYMENT ENHANCER INCENTIVE₹2,000

State Government incentive per person, per month.

TRANSITION ALLOWANCE₹2,000

Per month during the three-month transition period from apprenticeship to job.

Employment transition

The apprenticeship may be converted into a job after three months, subject to mutual agreement between the apprentice and host organisation.

Participating host network

Applicants may indicate preferred host organisations or sectors, including Government Departments, Urban Local Bodies, State Government Institutions and Private Institutions. ASAP has 1,472 recruitment partners onboarded.

NODAL AGENCY

ASAP administration.

Programme coordination, candidate processing, placement, stipend administration, compliance and reporting.

Programme responsibilities

  • Manage registration, selection and placement.
  • Disburse stipend with State incentive.
  • Monitor compliance and workplace verification.
  • Maintain post-specific progress index.
  • Address grievances and coordinate escalation.
